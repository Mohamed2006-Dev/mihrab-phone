# Geography data
Derived from GeoNames countryInfo.txt and cities15000.zip, retrieved 2026-11-01.
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/

Changes: removed obsolete CS/AN entries; grouped by country; selected Arabic alternate names where available; preserved the application’s original Arabic city entries and coordinates; attached IANA zones from nearest GeoNames city for those legacy entries.
249 country/territory options, including XK as supplied by GeoNames. At the user’s request, the IL option is removed and its city records are grouped under Palestine (PS); coordinates and time zones are preserved.
The city extract covers population >15000 or capitals, not every settlement.
Territories with no cities in this extract remain selectable for manual entry; no coordinates are invented.
Country labels use Unicode CLDR Arabic names via Intl.DisplayNames except preserved legacy labels.
All data is supplied as-is; the original source does not warrant completeness or accuracy.
