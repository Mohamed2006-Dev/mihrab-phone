Mihrab Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

I am Mohamed Reda Ben Omar, the developer of Mihrab for Android. I dedicate Mihrab to my late grandfather as an ongoing charity and built it to support daily prayers and devotional reading. This policy explains how the app uses data and what you can control.

Data on your device

Mihrab stores appearance, language and notification settings, the selected region and coordinates, prayer times and verification results, devotional reading and memorization progress, custom Adhkar, Hadith display history, followed channels, and cached data on your device. It displays local network and storage usage and device information needed to operate. Reading the bundled translations of Quran meanings works locally.

I do not require an account or run a cloud service to store or sync your activity. I do not sell your data or include advertising, marketing analytics or automatic crash reporting to me.

Location and prayer times

Where device location is available, Mihrab can ask for approximate location to calculate prayer times and refresh the region while you use the app. Mihrab does not request precise or background location permission. You can select a city or enter coordinates manually and revoke permission in Android settings.

Online verification may contact Morocco’s Ministry of Habous and Islamic Affairs (habous.gov.ma) for supported regions. When using AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com), Mihrab sends the selected coordinates, whether provided by the device or entered manually, and the requested date or time, time zone and calculation method. You can turn off online verification and continue using local calculations.

Local sync and camera

If you enable pairing, your phone and the computer you pair it with exchange the data groups you choose, in the direction you configure. The options include followed channels, custom reminders, devotional reading settings and progress, interface language and appearance, and sending the phone location to the computer. The devices communicate directly over your local network using HTTPS, with the computer’s identity checked against the pairing certificate fingerprint. Both devices retain the pairing information needed to keep the connection working.

On supported devices, with your permission, the camera reads the pairing QR code. The image is processed on the device to read the code. I do not receive camera images. You can use a text pairing code instead. You can unlink a device in the sync settings. Synced copies may remain on devices that received them.

External services and reports

Mihrab uses HadeethEnc to load Hadith and explanations by Hadith ID or online search query. It requests channel data, feeds and thumbnails from YouTube and its image servers using channel or video IDs or links you add. The services your device connects to receive your IP address and standard connection metadata. When you open an external link, that service’s privacy policy applies.

If you choose to open a report in GitHub or Google Forms, the app opens a link prefilled with the report category, description and any note you add. If you choose to include technical context, the link may also contain the app version, operating system, language and selected Quran recitation. Those fields reach the service when the link loads, including before you confirm submission. Reports you post to the public GitHub repository are visible to anyone. If you email me, I receive your email address and message. Avoid including sensitive information in reports.

Notifications, images and sharing

Mihrab schedules prayer, devotional reading and lesson reminders on the device. It may use exact alarms and reschedule after startup. Android settings control notification permissions and lock-screen display. If you save a Hadith image, it goes into your photo gallery. Older Android versions may require storage write permission. When you share or copy content, it goes to the app you select or the system clipboard.

Data retention and security

Local data stays on your device until you delete it in Mihrab, clear app data in Android settings, or uninstall. Copies may remain on paired devices or in Android backups and device transfers, depending on your settings. Saved Hadith images remain in your gallery. Uninstalling does not delete data retained by external services.

Android protects the app’s storage area. The services listed above and local sync use HTTPS. I keep messages and reports I receive as needed to follow up or resolve the issue. Third-party copies are subject to their own policies. I do not ask for ages or identifying information about children.
